## What is the Blipdeck
Do you like collecting physical retro games but still prefer emulating them on your PC? Then you should build yourself a Blipdeck.

Blipdeck is a just small box that contains:

* A card reader
* A USB Hub
* A DVD Reader
* An NFC Reader
